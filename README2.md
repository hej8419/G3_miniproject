1. This feature helps learners understand and remember travel words because pictures, example sentences, and scene backgrounds connect each word to meaningful situations.
2. This feature helps learners recall word meanings and spellings because cumulative quizzes bring back words from earlier scenes and provide repeated retrieval practice.
3. This feature helps learners use travel words in context because filling in blanks in a short travel story requires them to connect word meanings with their use in sentences.
4. This feature helps learners track their progress and review earlier words because passport stamps mark completed scenes and return trips provide delayed retrieval practice.
